Our Story

Built for the Future of Telecom

Telecomnet IT Solutions Pvt Ltd was founded with a singular mission: to bridge the gap between complex telecom infrastructure and the intelligent digital services modern subscribers expect.

What began as a focused M-VAS provider has evolved into an end-to-end telecom technology company — engineering AI-powered platforms, cloud-native applications, and conversational AI systems deployed across leading mobile networks including Orange, MTN, and Airtel.

Today, our engineering teams across India and Africa build production-grade platforms processing over 10 million transactions daily — combining traditional telecom protocols with modern AI, ML, and cloud-native architectures.

"To be the forefront innovator transforming the digital landscape through unparalleled value-added services, cutting-edge mobile applications, and advanced telecom platforms."
